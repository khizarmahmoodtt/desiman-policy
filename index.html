Privacy Policy

Desiman: The Chronicles (the "Game") by PLAYGRID. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game is for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

1. Information you give the Game

  • Nickname. A name you choose. Please do not use your real name. It is shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Country. Shown next to your nickname on the leaderboard.
  • Date of birth. Used only to check that you are 13 or over. It is stored on your device and is never sent to our servers.

2. Information the Game sends to our leaderboard

If the online leaderboard is available, the Game sends: your nickname, your country, your best score and level reached, and a random anonymous player ID that the Game creates on your device. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files.

Our leaderboard is hosted by Supabase (supabase.com). Your IP address is visible to the hosting provider when your device connects, as with any online service.

3. Information stored on your device

Your profile, settings (volume, control layout, graphics), saved progress and best score are stored locally on your device so the Game can remember them. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes them.

4. Advertising

The Game may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required by law, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. How we use information

  • To run the leaderboard and show your rank.
  • To keep the Game working and prevent cheating or abuse (for example, rejecting impossible scores).
  • To show ads that help keep the Game running.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active. To have your leaderboard entry removed, email us the nickname and country you used and we will delete it. You can also clear the Game's data in Android settings to remove everything stored on your device.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and blocks sign-up for players younger than 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the leaderboard, contact us and we will remove the entry.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
