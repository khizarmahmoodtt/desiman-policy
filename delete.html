Delete Your Data

Desiman: The Chronicles by PLAYGRID. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

The Game has no accounts or logins. The only data we hold about you on our servers is your online leaderboard entry. This page explains how to have it deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to Desiman.comics@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Desiman data.
  2. In the email, include the nickname and country you used on the leaderboard.
  3. We will delete your entry and reply to confirm, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard nickname
  • Your country
  • Your best score and level reached
  • Your anonymous player ID linked to that entry

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which handles its own data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Data on your own device

Your profile, settings, saved progress and date of birth (used only for the 13+ age check) are stored only on your phone and never sent to us. To remove them, uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Desiman > Storage > Clear data).

See also our Privacy Policy.
